Read brain.md first.

Project: hunter2 (this repo). A browser game in C, compiled to wasm, rendered with WebGPU through our own thin JS bridge. Engine written from scratch. The game may end up 2D or 3D, not decided yet.

The main goal is learning: we work through Ian Millington's "Game Physics Engine Development" chapter by chapter and build its physics engine together in src/phys/. The physics comes first, the game second. Second goal: everything lightning quick, which is why we stay with C + wasm + our own WebGPU bridge. Rendering, camera and tooling only exist to see and check the physics: keep them cheap, do not let them grow into their own project.

Setup: run ./setup.sh first. It checks the tools (it test builds a tiny wasm), clones the book's source code, extracts the book text and sets the git identity, and only does what is missing. Tools come from PATH, except clang falls back to Homebrew's LLVM when the clang on PATH cannot build wasm32 (find_clang.sh; override with CLANG, WASM_LD). If it reports MISSING, fix what you can (tools: ask before installing) and ask me for the rest. Where we are (2026-10-31): physics covers book ch 2-5, ch 6.1 to 6.2.4, plus our own contacts. Ch 5 force generators (src/phys/force.c): ForceGen is a kind enum + union with a switch, and a ForceRegistry over a caller owned array. Ch 6 springs are four more kinds, one per variant (my pick): FORCE_SPRING {other, k, rest}, FORCE_ANCHORED_SPRING {anchor, k, rest}, FORCE_BUNGEE, FORCE_ANCHORED_BUNGEE, all through one helper force_hooke. No abs on (length - rest) like the book's ParticleSpring has (with it a compressed spring pulls). Buoyancy is FORCE_BUOYANCY {water_height, depth, volume, density, gravity, drag}, with the book's three bugs fixed (missing g, flipped depth sign, precedence; see the focus file) and water drag scaled by the share under water. Collisions are contacts (src/phys/collide.c, our take on ch 7). A step runs: forces, particle_integrate_velocity, find contacts, particle_contacts_warm, particle_contacts_solve, particle_integrate_position, particle_contacts_bounce. Four demos, each with its own title color (Demo.title_color): "balls" (ch 3, Erasmos Balls), "cloud" (ch 5, Gravity Cloud), "bridge" (ch 6, Rope Bridge: a deck of balls on spring links, hung by spring or bungee cables, Space drops a heavy ball, R resets, springs drawn as lines colored by stretch; I like its sliders live with no reset, leave them) and "pool" (ch 6, Ball Pool: the balls box with water, mass ~ r^2 so small balls sink and big ones float, water level, density and drag live). The bridge blows up between link k 7000 and 8000: that is 6.3's topic. compile_flags.txt for clangd is in. Everything is committed, not pushed (push over SSH, ask me first).
