Where we are (2026-10-31): physics covers book ch 2-5 plus our own contacts. Ch 5 force generators (src/phys/force.c): ForceGen is a kind enum + union with a switch (FORCE_GRAVITY, FORCE_DRAG, FORCE_ATTRACT, FORCE_EXPLODE), and a ForceRegistry over a caller owned array. Collisions are contacts (src/phys/collide.c, our take on ch 7). A step runs: forces, particle_integrate_velocity, find contacts, particle_contacts_warm, particle_contacts_solve, particle_integrate_position, particle_contacts_bounce. Contacts only add impulses along the line of centers, so momentum and angular momentum stay exact. The book's way (integrate, then push positions apart) made the gravity cloud spin faster and faster; that bug is fixed. Contacts are speculative (a pair may close its gap this step, not more), warm started from last step, and overlap past a 0.5 cm slop is opened by velocity. Two demos: "balls" (ch 3, a box of up to 2000 balls, the walls are plane contacts) and "cloud" (ch 5, Gravity Cloud, bodies pulling on each other with collisions). Everything is committed and pushed.
